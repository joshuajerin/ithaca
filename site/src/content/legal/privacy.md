# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how **Ithaca, Inc., a Delaware corporation** ("Ithaca", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use the Ithaca platform and related services (the "Service"). For privacy or data-protection inquiries, contact [joshuaxjerin@gmail.com](mailto:joshuaxjerin@gmail.com).

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account and identity data** — when you sign up, our authentication provider Clerk processes your name, email address, and authentication metadata on our behalf.
- **Usage and research data** — research sessions, prompts, StrategySpecs, tool calls, backtest runs, and the resulting traces. Every tool call is recorded to power the replayable "glass box."
- **Technical data** — IP address, device/browser information, and request logs collected for security and reliability.
- **Analytics data** — aggregate usage metrics collected via Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

We do **not** intentionally collect special-category personal data, and you should not submit it through the Service.

## 2. How we use information

- To provide, operate, secure, and improve the Service.
- To authenticate you and maintain your account.
- To record and replay research traces for transparency and auditability.
- To monitor for abuse, enforce our [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use), and comply with legal obligations.
- To communicate with you about the Service.

## 3. Legal bases (GDPR/UK GDPR)

Where applicable, we rely on: **performance of a contract** (to provide the Service), **legitimate interests** (security, abuse prevention, product improvement), **consent** (for non-essential analytics cookies), and **legal obligation**.

## 4. How we share information

We share personal information with **subprocessors** who help us run the Service — including Clerk (authentication), Vercel (hosting and analytics), and DigitalOcean (compute, database, and storage), as well as market-data providers. See the full [Subprocessors](/subprocessors) list. We may also disclose information to comply with law, enforce our Terms, or protect rights and safety. We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 5. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, then for a reasonable period to meet legal, security, and audit requirements. Research traces and StrategySpecs may be retained as immutable records for reproducibility; contact us to request deletion subject to those constraints.

## 6. Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including tenant-scoped access controls and auditing of every API/MCP call. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA) may request disclosure and deletion and may opt out of "sale"/"sharing" (we do not sell or share for cross-context behavioral advertising). To exercise rights, contact [joshuaxjerin@gmail.com](mailto:joshuaxjerin@gmail.com). You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

## 8. International transfers

We may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for international transfers.

## 9. Children

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
