# Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how **Ithaca, Inc., a Delaware corporation** ("we") uses cookies and similar technologies (local storage, pixels) on the Ithaca websites and application (the "Service"). It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies (such as browser local storage) perform comparable functions. They can be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by our providers).

## 2. Types of cookies we use

| Category | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Strictly necessary** | Authentication, session management, and security. The Service cannot function without these. | Clerk session and authentication cookies |
| **Analytics / performance** | Aggregate, privacy-preserving usage and performance measurement to help us improve the Service. | Vercel Analytics, Vercel Speed Insights |

We do **not** use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 3. Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in and using the Service. Where required by law, we request consent before setting non-essential (analytics) cookies and honor your choice.

## 4. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy as our use of cookies evolves. The "Last updated" date reflects the latest revision.
