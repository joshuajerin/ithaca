# Third-Party Data Sources

Doomberg, operated by **Ithaca, Inc., a Delaware corporation**, integrates market and reference data from third-party providers. This page describes those providers and the limitations that apply to data made available through the Service. Data is provided on an **"as is"** basis for research and simulated ("paper") use only; see our [Risk Disclosures](/disclosures).

## 1. Alpaca

Consolidated U.S. equities market data (SIP tapes / NBBO) is sourced via **Alpaca**. This data is provided for your internal research and paper-trading use within the Service and may not be redistributed, resold, or used for live order routing except as permitted by Alpaca's terms.

## 2. Databento

Normalized professional market-data feeds are sourced via **Databento**. Use is subject to Databento's licensing terms. You may not redistribute or extract bulk raw data from the Service.

## 3. Sharadar

Point-in-time, survivorship-bias-free fundamentals and reference history are sourced via **Sharadar**. This data supports research and backtesting and may not be redistributed outside the Service.

## 4. Financial Modeling Prep (FMP)

Supplementary reference and fundamentals data may be sourced via **Financial Modeling Prep (FMP)**. Use is subject to FMP's terms and is limited to research and simulation within the Service.
