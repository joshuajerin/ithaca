You may not use the Service, its data, or its outputs to plan, facilitate, or carry out:

- Market manipulation, spoofing, layering, wash trading, or any deceptive or fraudulent trading practice.
- Insider trading or trading on the basis of material non-public information.

The Service is for research and simulation; it does not authorize or enable live trading.

## 3. No malicious or harmful content

You may not upload or transmit malware, attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems or accounts, or use the Service to harm, harass, or infringe the rights of others.

We welcome good-faith security research. You may probe for vulnerabilities only against your own account/tenant, must not access or destroy other users' data, must not degrade the Service, and must report findings promptly to [joshuaxjerin@gmail.com](mailto:joshuaxjerin@gmail.com) before any disclosure.
